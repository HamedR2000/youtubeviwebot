✏️ Doodle Pals

Learn shapes and simple English words with Penny the Pencil!

Doodle Pals is an educational YouTube channel for children aged 2–6. In each short video Penny the Pencil draws a simple shape — a circle, a square, a triangle — that turns into a friendly character, and the English word is spelled out letter by letter.

All videos are original 2D animations with original music, made for kids and safe for families.
